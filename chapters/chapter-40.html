1 填空题
$\int_{0}^{1}\ln\dfrac{1}{1-x}\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
令 $t=\ln\frac{1}{1-x}$，则 $x=1-\mathrm{e}^{-t}$，故 $$\text{原式}=\int_{0}^{+\infty}t\mathrm{d}(1-\mathrm{e}^{-t})=\int_{0}^{+\infty}t\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-t}\mathrm{d}t=1.$$
最终答案1
2 解答题
求 $\displaystyle\int\frac{x+2}{(2x+1)(x^{2}+x+1)}\,\mathrm{d}x$.
设 $\frac{x+2}{(2x+1)(x^2+x+1)}=\frac{A}{2x+1}+\frac{Bx+D}{x^2+x+1}$，通分后可得 $$x+2=A(x^2+x+1)+(Bx+D)(2x+1),$$ 代入特值 $x=-\frac{1}{2}$，$x=0$，$x=1$，得 $A=2$，$D=0$，$B=-1$，故 $$\begin{aligned}\text{原式}&=\int\left(\frac{2}{2x+1}-\frac{x}{x^2+x+1}\right)\mathrm{d}x\\&=\ln|2x+1|-\int\frac{x}{x^2+x+1}\mathrm{d}x\\&=\ln|2x+1|-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{(2x+1)-1}{x^2+x+1}\mathrm{d}x\\&=\ln|2x+1|-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(x^2+x+1)}{x^2+x+1}+\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\left(x+\frac{1}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2}\\&=\ln|2x+1|-\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+x+1)+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\arctan\frac{2x+1}{\sqrt{3}}+C.\end{aligned}$$
3 填空题
$\int_{1}^{\mathrm{e}}\cos(\ln x)\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
令 $\ln x=t$，则 $x=\mathrm{e}^t$，故 $$\begin{aligned}\text{原式}&=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{e}^t\cos t\mathrm{d}t\\&=\frac{1}{2}\left(\mathrm{e}^t\cos t+\mathrm{e}^t\sin t\right)\Big|_{0}^{1}\\&=\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}(\cos 1+\sin 1)-\frac{1}{2}.\end{aligned}$$
最终答案$\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}(\cos 1+\sin 1)-\frac{1}{2}$
4 解答题
设函数 $f(x)$ 满足方程 $xf(x)+f(1-x)=x^{2}$，求 $\int f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$.
以 $1-x$ 代换原方程中的 $x$，得 $(1-x)f(1-x)+f(x)=(1-x)^2$. 此方程与原方程联立，解得 $f(x)=\frac{x^3-2x+1}{x^2-x+1}$. 于是 $$\begin{aligned}\int f(x)\mathrm{d}x&=\int\frac{x^3-2x+1}{x^2-x+1}\mathrm{d}x=\int\left(x+1+\frac{-2x}{x^2-x+1}\right)\mathrm{d}x\\&=\frac{1}{2}x^2+x+\int\frac{-(2x-1)-1}{x^2-x+1}\mathrm{d}x\\&=\frac{1}{2}x^2+x-\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(x^2-x+1)}{x^2-x+1}-\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2}\\&=\frac{1}{2}x^2+x-\ln(x^2-x+1)-\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\arctan\frac{2x-1}{\sqrt{3}}+C.\end{aligned}$$
5 填空题
$\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{n}^{n+1}2^{-\sqrt{x}}\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
$$\begin{aligned}\text{原式}&=\int_{1}^{+\infty}2^{-\sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{\sqrt{x}=t}\int_{1}^{+\infty}2^{-t}\cdot 2t\mathrm{d}t\\&=\frac{-2}{\ln 2}\int_{1}^{+\infty}t\mathrm{d}(2^{-t})=-\frac{2}{\ln 2}\left(t\cdot 2^{-t}\Big|_{1}^{+\infty}-\int_{1}^{+\infty}2^{-t}\mathrm{d}t\right)\\&=-\frac{2}{\ln 2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2^{-t}}{\ln 2}\Big|_{1}^{+\infty}\right)\\&=\frac{1}{\ln 2}+\frac{1}{(\ln 2)^2}.\end{aligned}$$
最终答案$\frac{1}{\ln 2}+\frac{1}{(\ln 2)^2}$
6 填空题
已知 $f(x)$ 是连续的偶函数，且 $\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=2$，则 $\int_{0}^{2}xf(1-x)\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
令 $x=1+t$，由 $f(x)$ 是连续的偶函数，且 $\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}x=2$，得 $$\int_{0}^{2}xf(1-x)\mathrm{d}x=\int_{-1}^{1}(1+t)f(-t)\mathrm{d}t=2\int_{0}^{1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t=4.$$
最终答案4
7 填空题
已知 $f(x)$ 连续，$f(x^{2}+1)-f(x^{2})=x\,(x>0)$，$\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=1$，则 $\int_{1}^{2}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
令 $F(x)=\int_{x^2}^{x^2+1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t\,(x>0)$，则 $F'(x)=2x[f(x^2+1)-f(x^2)]=2x^{-1}$，则 $$\int_{x^2}^{x^2+1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t=F(x)=\frac{2}{3}x^3+C.$$ 取极限 $x\to 0^+$，得 $\int_{0}^{1}f(t)\mathrm{d}t=C$，即 $C=1$，则 $F(x)=\frac{2}{3}x^3+1\,(x>0)$，故 $$\int_{1}^{2}f(x)\mathrm{d}x=F(1)=\frac{2}{3}+1=\frac{5}{3}.$$
最终答案$\frac{5}{3}$
8 解答题
设 $f(t)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}t\,|t-x|\,\mathrm{d}x$，求 $\int_{-1}^{2}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t$.
由 $f(t)=\int_{0}^{1}t\,|t-x|\,\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal[\mathrm{d}x=-\mathrm{d}u]{t-x=u}\int_{1}^{t-1}t\,|u|\,(-\mathrm{d}u)=t\int_{t-1}^{t}|u|\,\mathrm{d}u$，故 当 $t\geqslant 1$ 时，$f(t)=t\cdot\left.\frac{1}{2}u^2\right|_{t-1}^{t}=t\cdot\left(t-\frac{1}{2}\right)=t^2-\frac{t}{2}$； 当 $0<t<1$ 时，$f(t)=t\cdot\left.\frac{1}{2}u^2\right|_{t-1}^{0}+t\cdot\left.\frac{1}{2}u^2\right|_{0}^{t}=t^3-t^2+\frac{t}{2}$； 当 $t\leqslant 0$ 时，$f(t)=t\cdot\left.\frac{1}{2}u^2\right|_{t}^{t-1}=-t^2+\frac{t}{2}$. 所以有 $$\begin{aligned}\int_{-1}^{2}f(t)\mathrm{d}t&=\int_{-1}^{0}\left(\frac{t}{2}-t^2\right)\mathrm{d}t+\int_{0}^{1}\left(t^3-t^2+\frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t+\int_{1}^{2}\left(t^2-\frac{t}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}t\\&=\left.\left(\frac{1}{4}t^2-\frac{1}{3}t^3\right)\right|_{-1}^{0}+\left.\left(\frac{1}{4}t^4-\frac{1}{3}t^3+\frac{1}{4}t^2\right)\right|_{0}^{1}+\left.\left(\frac{1}{3}t^3-\frac{1}{4}t^2\right)\right|_{1}^{2}\\&=-\frac{7}{12}+\frac{1}{6}+\frac{19}{12}=\frac{7}{6}.\end{aligned}$$

(A)$-2$(B)$-1$(C)$1$(D)$2$

10 填空题
已知 $f'(x)=\arctan(x-1)^{2}$，$f(0)=0$，则 $\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
$$\begin{aligned}f(x)&=\int_{0}^{x}f'(t)\mathrm{d}t+f(0)=\int_{0}^{x}\arctan(t-1)^2\mathrm{d}t,\\\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}x&=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{0}^{x}\arctan(t-1)^2\mathrm{d}t\\&\xlongequal{\text{交换次序}}\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}t\int_{t}^{1}\arctan(t-1)^2\mathrm{d}x\\&=\int_{0}^{1}(1-t)\arctan(t-1)^2\mathrm{d}t\\&\xlongequal{(1-t)^2=u}\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\arctan u\,\mathrm{d}u=\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{4}\ln 2.\end{aligned}$$
最终答案$\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{4}\ln 2$
11 填空题
设 $g(x)=x^{2}$，$g[f(x)]=-x^{2}+2x+3$，且 $f(x)>0$，则 $\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{1}{f(x)}\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
由题意，$f^2(x)=-x^2+2x+3$，且 $f(x)>0$，所以 $$f(x)=\sqrt{-x^2+2x+3}=\sqrt{4-(x-1)^2},$$ $$\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{f(x)}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{4-(x-1)^2}}\,\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{x-1=t}\int_{-1}^{0}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2^2-t^2}}\,\mathrm{d}t=\left.\arcsin\frac{t}{2}\right|_{-1}^{0}=\frac{\pi}{6}.$$
最终答案$\frac{\pi}{6}$
12 解答题
求 $\int_{-1}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{x})\,\mathrm{d}x$.
$$\begin{aligned}\int_{-1}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^x)\,\mathrm{d}x&=\int_{-1}^{0}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^x)\,\mathrm{d}x+\int_{0}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^x)\,\mathrm{d}x\\&=\int_{1}^{0}(-x)\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{-x})(-\mathrm{d}x)+\int_{0}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^x)\,\mathrm{d}x\\&=-\int_{0}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^{-x})\,\mathrm{d}x+\int_{0}^{1}x\ln(1+\mathrm{e}^x)\,\mathrm{d}x\\&=\int_{0}^{1}x\ln\frac{1+\mathrm{e}^x}{1+\mathrm{e}^{-x}}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}x\ln\mathrm{e}^x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}x^2\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{3}.\end{aligned}$$ 【注】原函数不易求的非奇非偶函数在对称区间上的积分，可采用此题方法处理.
13 解答题
求 $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x+1)(x^{2}+1)}$.
注意到 $\frac{1}{(x+1)(x^2+1)}=\frac{1}{2(x+1)}-\frac{x-1}{2(x^2+1)}$，故 $$\begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x+1)(x^2+1)}&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x+1}-\frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}(x^2+1)}{x^2+1}+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2+1}\\&=\left.\frac{1}{2}\ln(x+1)\right|_{0}^{1}-\left.\frac{1}{4}\ln(x^2+1)\right|_{0}^{1}+\left.\frac{1}{2}\arctan x\right|_{0}^{1}\\&=\frac{\pi}{8}+\frac{1}{4}\ln 2.\end{aligned}$$
14 解答题
设 $F(x)>0$ 为 $\mathbf{R}$ 上的连续可导函数，$F(0)=\sqrt{\pi}$，且 $F(x)F'(x)=\dfrac{\cos x}{2\sin^{2}x+\cos^{2}x}$，求 $F(x)$.
分离变量得 $F(x)\mathrm{d}[F(x)]=\frac{\mathrm{d}(\sin x)}{1+\sin^2 x}$，故 $F^2(x)=2\arctan(\sin x)+C$. 将 $F(0)=\sqrt{\pi}$ 代入得 $C=\pi$，又 $F(x)>0$，故 $F(x)=\sqrt{2\arctan(\sin x)+\pi}$.

16 解答题
求 $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin^{2}x+3\cos^{2}x}$.
$$\begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin^2 x+3\cos^2 x}&=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\cos^2 x(3+\tan^2 x)}\xlongequal{t=\tan x}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{3+t^2}\\&=\left.\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\arctan\frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\right|_{0}^{1}=\frac{\pi}{6\sqrt{3}}.\end{aligned}$$
17 解答题
求 $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{3}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{\left|2x-x^{2}\right|}}$.
$x=2$ 为奇点，应将积分区间分为两部分，即 $$\begin{aligned}\int_{1}^{3}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{|2x-x^2|}}&=\int_{1}^{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{2x-x^2}}+\int_{2}^{3}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x^2-2x}}\\&=\int_{1}^{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-(x-1)^2}}+\int_{2}^{3}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{(x-1)^2-1}}\\&=\left.\arcsin(x-1)\right|_{1}^{2}+\left.\ln[x-1+\sqrt{(x-1)^2-1}]\right|_{2}^{3}\\&=\frac{\pi}{2}+\ln(2+\sqrt{3}).\end{aligned}$$
18 填空题
设 $n$ 为非负整数，则 $\int_{0}^{1}x^{2}\ln^{n}x\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
记 $$\begin{aligned}a_n&=\int_{0}^{1}x^2\ln^n x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}\ln^n x\,\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{1}{3}x^3\right)\\&=\left.\frac{1}{3}x^3\ln^n x\right|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{3}x^3\cdot n\ln^{n-1}x\cdot\frac{1}{x}\,\mathrm{d}x\\&=-\frac{n}{3}\int_{0}^{1}x^2\ln^{n-1}x\,\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{n}{3}a_{n-1},n=1,2,\cdots, \end{aligned}$$ 于是 $a_n=-\frac{n}{3}a_{n-1}=\left(-\frac{n}{3}\right)\left(-\frac{n-1}{3}\right)a_{n-2}=\cdots=\left(-\frac{n}{3}\right)\left(-\frac{n-1}{3}\right)\cdots\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)a_0$，又 $a_0=\int_{0}^{1}x^2\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{3}$，故 $a_n=\frac{(-1)^n}{3^{n+1}}n!$.
最终答案$\frac{(-1)^n}{3^{n+1}}n!$
19 选择题
设 $f(x)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\ln\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,\mathrm{d}y$，$0\leqslant x\leqslant 1$，则 $f_{+}(0)=（\quad）$.
(A)$-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$(B)$\dfrac{\pi}{2}$(C)$-\pi$(D)$\pi$
当 $0<x\leqslant 1$ 时， $$\begin{aligned}f(x)&=\left.y\ln\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,\right|_{y=0}^{y=1}-\int_{0}^{1}y\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\cdot\frac{2y}{2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\,\mathrm{d}y\\&=\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-\int_{0}^{1}\frac{y^2}{x^2+y^2}\,\mathrm{d}y\\&=\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^2+y^2-x^2}{x^2+y^2}\,\mathrm{d}y\\&=\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-1+\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\,\mathrm{d}y\\&=\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-1+x\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{1+\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)^2}\,\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\\&=\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-1+x\arctan\frac{1}{x}, \end{aligned}$$ 又 $f(0)=\int_{0}^{1}\ln y\,\mathrm{d}y=-1$，故 $$f_{+}'(0)=\lim_{x\to 0^+}\frac{f(x)-f(0)}{x-0}=\lim_{x\to 0^+}\frac{\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}-1+x\arctan\dfrac{1}{x}+1}{x}$$ $$\begin{aligned}&=\lim_{x\to 0^+}\frac{\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}}{x}+\lim_{x\to 0^+}\arctan\frac{1}{x}\\&=\lim_{x\to 0^+}\frac{\sqrt{x^2+1}-1}{x}+\frac{\pi}{2}=0+\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{2}.\end{aligned}$$
最终答案(B)
20 解答题
设 $|x|\leqslant 1$，求积分 $I(x)=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}|t-x|\,\mathrm{e}^{t^{2}}\mathrm{d}t$ 的最大值.
由题设知，积分变量 $t$ 与求导变量 $x$ 的取值在同一区间，不需要分情况讨论，故 $$\begin{aligned}I(x)&=\int_{-1}^{1}|t-x|\,\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t\\&=\int_{-1}^{x}(x-t)\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{x}^{1}(t-x)\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t\\&=x\int_{-1}^{x}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{-1}^{x}t\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{x}^{1}t\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t-x\int_{x}^{1}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t, \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned}I'(x)&=\int_{-1}^{x}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t+x\mathrm{e}^{2x}-x\mathrm{e}^{2x}-x\mathrm{e}^{2x}-\int_{x}^{1}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t+x\mathrm{e}^{2x}=\int_{-1}^{x}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{x}^{1}\mathrm{e}^{2t}\,\mathrm{d}t\\&=\mathrm{e}^{2x}-\frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{e}^2+\mathrm{e}^{-2})\xlongequal{\text{令}}0, \end{aligned}$$ 得 $x_0=\frac{1}{2}\ln\dfrac{\mathrm{e}^t+\mathrm{e}^{-t}}{2}$ 为唯一驻点，$I''(x)=2\mathrm{e}^{2x}>0$，故 $x_0=\frac{1}{2}\ln\dfrac{\mathrm{e}^2+\mathrm{e}^{-2}}{2}$ 为 $I(x)$ 在 $[-1,1]$ 上的最小值点，最大值只能在端点 $x=-1$，$x=1$ 处取得. 又 $$I(-1)=\frac{3}{4}\mathrm{e}^2+\frac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-2},\,I(1)=\frac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^2-\frac{5}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-2},$$ 所以 $[I(x)]_{\max}=I(-1)=\frac{3}{4}\mathrm{e}^2+\frac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-2}$.
21 解答题
设函数 $f(x)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}|t^{2}-x^{2}|\,\mathrm{d}t\,(x>0)$，求 $f'(x)$，并求 $f(x)$ 的最小值.
由题设知，积分变量 $t$ 与求导变量 $x$ 的取值不在同一区间，需要分情况讨论. 当 $0<x\leqslant 1$ 时，$f(x)=\int_{0}^{x}|t^2-x^2|\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{x}^{1}|t^2-x^2|\,\mathrm{d}t$ $$=\int_{0}^{x}(x^2-t^2)\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{x}^{1}(t^2-x^2)\,\mathrm{d}t=\frac{4}{3}x^3-x^2+\frac{1}{3};$$ 当 $x>1$ 时，$f(x)=\int_{0}^{1}(x^2-t^2)\,\mathrm{d}t=x^2-\frac{1}{3}.$ 所以 $$f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{4}{3}x^3-x^2+\dfrac{1}{3}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\[2mm]x^2-\dfrac{1}{3}, & x>1.\end{cases}$$ 而 $$f_{-}'(1)=\lim_{x\to 1^-}\frac{\dfrac{4}{3}x^3-x^2+\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{2}{3}}{x-1}=2,\,f_{+}'(1)=\lim_{x\to 1^+}\dfrac{x^2-\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{2}{3}}{x-1}=2,$$ 故 $$f'(x)=\begin{cases}4x^2-2x, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\[1mm]2x, & x>1.\end{cases}$$ 由 $f'(x)=0$ 得唯一驻点 $x=\frac{1}{2}$，又 $f'\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)>0$，从而 $x=\frac{1}{2}$ 为 $f(x)$ 的极小值点，即为最小值点，最小值为 $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{4}$.
22 解答题
设 $f(x)=x$，$g(x)=\begin{cases}\cos x, & x\leqslant\pi,\\ 0, & x>\pi,\end{cases}$ 求 $F(x)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}f(t)g(x-t)\,\mathrm{d}t\;(x\geqslant 0)$.
$$F(x)=\int_{0}^{x}f(t)g(x-t)\mathrm{d}t \xrightarrow{x-t=u} \int_{0}^{x}f(x-u)g(u)\mathrm{d}u=x\int_{0}^{x}g(u)\mathrm{d}u-\int_{0}^{x}ug(u)\mathrm{d}u.$$当 $0\leqslant x\leqslant \pi$ 时，$$F(x)=x\int_{0}^{x}\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u-\int_{0}^{x}u\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u=x\sin x-\int_{0}^{x}u\,\mathrm{d}(\sin u)=1-\cos x.$$当 $x>\pi$ 时，$$F(x)=x\Big[\int_{0}^{\pi}g(u)\mathrm{d}u+\int_{\pi}^{x}g(u)\mathrm{d}u\Big]-\Big[\int_{0}^{\pi}ug(u)\mathrm{d}u+\int_{\pi}^{x}ug(u)\mathrm{d}u\Big]=x\Big(\int_{0}^{\pi}\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u+\int_{\pi}^{x}0\,\mathrm{d}u\Big)-\Big(\int_{0}^{\pi}u\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u+\int_{\pi}^{x}0\,\mathrm{d}u\Big)=2.$$于是，$$F(x)=\begin{cases}1-\cos x, & 0\leqslant x\leqslant \pi,\\2, & x>\pi.\end{cases}$$
23 填空题
设 $y=f(x)=x\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-(xt)^{2}}\mathrm{d}t+x^{2}$，其在 $x=0$ 的某邻域内与 $x=g(y)$ 互为反函数，则 $g''(0)=$ ______.
由 $$x\int_{0}^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-(xt)^{2}}\mathrm{d}t \xrightarrow{\text{令 } xt=u} \int_{0}^{2x}\mathrm{e}^{-u^{2}}\mathrm{d}u,$$得 $$f(x)=\int_{0}^{2x}\mathrm{e}^{-u^{2}}\mathrm{d}u+x^{2},$$于是 $f'(x)=2\mathrm{e}^{-4x^{2}}+2x$，$f''(x)=-16x\mathrm{e}^{-4x^{2}}+2$，$f'(0)=2$，$f''(0)=2$，且 $f(0)=0$，故 $$g''(0)=\frac{-f''(0)}{[f'(0)]^{3}}=-\frac{1}{4}.$$
最终答案$-\dfrac{1}{4}$
24 解答题
$F(x)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}|\sin x-\sin t|\,\mathrm{d}t\;(x\geqslant 0)$ 在 $x\to 0^{+}$ 处的 2 次泰勒多项式为 $a+bx+cx^{2}$，求 $a$，$b$，$c$ 的值.
当 $x\to 0^{+}$ 时，$$F(x)=\int_{0}^{x}(\sin x-\sin t)\mathrm{d}t+\int_{x}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(\sin t-\sin x)\mathrm{d}t=x\sin x+\cos x-1+\cos x-\sin x\cdot\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\Big)=\Big(2x-\frac{\pi}{2}\Big)\sin x+2\cos x-1.$$方法一 直接展开. $\sin x=x+o(x^{2})$，$\cos x=1-\dfrac{1}{2}x^{2}+o(x^{2})$，则 $$F(x)=\Big(2x-\frac{\pi}{2}\Big)[x+o(x^{2})]+2\Big[1-\frac{1}{2}x^{2}+o(x^{2})\Big]-1=1-\frac{\pi}{2}x+x^{2}+o(x^{2}).$$因此 $a=1$，$b=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$，$c=1$.方法二 由 $F'(x)=2\sin x+\Big(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)\cos x-2\sin x=\Big(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)\cos x$，$F''(x)=2\cos x-\Big(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)\sin x$，故 $F(0)=1$，$F'_{+}(0)=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$，$F''_{+}(0)=2$，$$F(x)=F(0)+F'_{+}(0)x+\frac{F''_{+}(0)}{2!}x^{2}+\cdots=1-\frac{\pi}{2}x+x^{2}+\cdots,$$即 $a=1$，$b=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$，$c=1$.
25 解答题
求定积分 $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{3x^{2}-2x-1}}$.
当 $x\in(1,2)$ 时，$$x\sqrt{3x^{2}-2x-1}=x\sqrt{(3x+1)(x-1)}=x(x-1)\sqrt{\frac{3x+1}{x-1}},$$令 $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{3x+1}{x-1}}$，有 $\dfrac{3x+1}{x-1}=t^{2}$，解得 $x=\dfrac{t^{2}+1}{t^{2}-3}=1+\dfrac{4}{t^{2}-3}$，则 $\mathrm{d}x=-\dfrac{8t}{(t^{2}-3)^{2}}\mathrm{d}t$.于是 $$\int_{1}^{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{3x^{2}-2x-1}}=\int_{1}^{2}\frac{1}{x(x-1)\sqrt{\dfrac{3x+1}{x-1}}}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{\sqrt{7}}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{\dfrac{t^{2}+1}{t^{2}-3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{t^{2}-3}\cdot t}\cdot\Big(-\frac{8t}{(t^{2}-3)^{2}}\Big)\mathrm{d}t=\int_{\sqrt{7}}^{+\infty}\frac{2}{1+t^{2}}\,\mathrm{d}t=2\arctan t\Big|_{\sqrt{7}}^{+\infty}=\pi-2\arctan\sqrt{7}.$$
26 选择题
反常积分 $\displaystyle\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x（\quad）$.
(A)取值为正(B)取值为负(C)取值为 0(D)发散
可利用分部积分法直接计算积分，因为 $\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\sin x=\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\cos x=0$，有 $$I=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\mathrm{d}(\sin x)=\mathrm{e}^{-x}\sin x\Big|_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}+\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\sin x\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x=0-\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\cdot(-1)-\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\mathrm{d}(\cos x)=\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}-\mathrm{e}^{-x}\cos x\Big|_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}-\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{+\infty}\cos x\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x=\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}-I,$$得 $I=\dfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}>0$，故选(A).
最终答案(A)
27 选择题
设 $f(x)=\begin{cases}x, & 0\leqslant x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & \text{其他},\end{cases}$ $g(x)=\begin{cases}1, & 0\leqslant x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & \text{其他},\end{cases}$ 则 $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t-x)g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t=（\quad）$.
(A)$\begin{cases}\dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{2}, & -1\leqslant x\leqslant 0,\\ \dfrac{x^{2}-1}{2}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & |x|>1\end{cases}$(B)$\begin{cases}\dfrac{(1-x)^{2}}{2}, & -1\leqslant x\leqslant 0,\\ \dfrac{1-x^{2}}{2}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & |x|>1\end{cases}$(C)$\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^{2}-1}{2}, & -1\leqslant x\leqslant 0,\\ \dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{2}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & |x|>1\end{cases}$(D)$\begin{cases}\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{2}, & -1\leqslant x\leqslant 0,\\ \dfrac{(1-x)^{2}}{2}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\ 0, & |x|>1\end{cases}$
$I=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t-x)g(t)\mathrm{d}t=\int_{0}^{1}f(t-x)\mathrm{d}t \xrightarrow{t-x=u} \int_{-x}^{1-x}f(u)\mathrm{d}u.$当 $-x>1$，即 $x<-1$ 时，$I=0$；当 $-x+1<0$，即 $x>1$ 时，$I=0$；当 $0\leqslant -x\leqslant 1$，即 $-1\leqslant x\leqslant 0$ 时，$I=\int_{-x}^{1}u\,\mathrm{d}u=\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{2}$；当 $0\leqslant -x+1<1$，即 $0<x\leqslant 1$ 时，$I=\int_{0}^{-x+1}u\,\mathrm{d}u=\dfrac{(1-x)^{2}}{2}$.故 $$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t-x)g(t)\mathrm{d}t=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{2}, & -1\leqslant x\leqslant 0,\\[2mm]\dfrac{(1-x)^{2}}{2}, & 0<x\leqslant 1,\\[2mm]0, & |x|>1.\end{cases}$$
最终答案(D)
28 填空题
$\displaystyle\int_{0}^{+\infty}\frac{x\ln x}{1+x^{4}}\,\mathrm{d}x=$ ______.
$$\int_{0}^{+\infty}\frac{x\ln x}{1+x^{4}}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x\ln x}{1+x^{4}}\,\mathrm{d}x+\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{x\ln x}{1+x^{4}}\,\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{\text{记}}I_{1}+I_{2},$$其中 $I_{1},I_{2}$ 收敛.而 $$I_{1}\xlongequal{x=\frac{1}{t}}\int_{+\infty}^{1}\frac{\dfrac{1}{t}(-\ln t)}{1+\dfrac{1}{t^{4}}}\cdot\Big(-\frac{1}{t^{2}}\Big)\mathrm{d}t=-\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{t\ln t}{1+t^{4}}\,\mathrm{d}t=-I_{2},$$故原式 $=I_{1}+I_{2}=0$.
最终答案0
